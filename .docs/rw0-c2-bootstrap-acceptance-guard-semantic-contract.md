# RW0-C2 Bootstrap Acceptance-Guard Semantic Contract

## Status and owner split

Classification: `ACCEPTED TARGET CONTRACT`, with physical implementation a
`REOPENED FUTURE SEAM`. This explicitly supersedes accepted-target mutable-latch
requirements; it does not change current implemented behavior. Historical latch
material remains historical/proof precursor evidence. Tests and reports are
projections, not authority. The operating model remains composition authority.

This is the sole guard semantic owner. The durable-decision owner
`.docs/rw0-c2-durable-progression-decision-semantic-contract.md` alone owns command
and basis grammar, currentness, V14/V15, matrices, five non-cancel branches,
decision/version/head semantics, mutation identity and canonical command digest,
result/receipt roles, branch-neutral exact replay, one whole-family recovery
classifier, no-write/block/unresolved, caller/model non-authority, and terminal
separation. This guard owner owns lineage, scope admission, applicability,
continuity, sole-active authority, cross-program ceiling, eligibility, immutable
acceptance-fact membership, non-replenishment, and guard-facing terminal/ES edges.
It owns no full future DTO schemas, separate receipt/replay/recovery, physical
storage, standing-policy issuance, execution admission/runtime, Git, or publication.
The accepted-target private schema IDs, canonicalization, identity/digest DAG,
branch graph roles, and versioned receipt profile are owned only by
`.docs/rw0-c2-private-value-versioning.md`. That value owner is subordinate to
this guard's lineage/admission/current external continuity and sole-active
semantics; it selects no physical representation and does not make the B1
legacy pair guard proof or a terminal consumer.

The restricted, unimplemented first RW0-E live support profile is
`.docs/rw0-c2-first-live-proof-posture.md` (`rw0-c2-single-realization-proof-v1`).
It owns the external incarnation-enrollment and managed-custody obligations
for that proof only. It neither changes this guard's universal current-posture
or acceptance-only unclaimed-scope rules nor makes single-realization custody
a permanent Coordinator architecture requirement.

## Lineage, admission, and permanence

One scope belongs to one explicitly standing-policy-admitted Coordinator authority lineage.
Option A is the accepted lineage policy. An independent lineage needs an explicit
independent standing-policy admission act. A deployment, machine, process,
database, database file, repository, program, attempt, candidate, command, parent
grant, restore, rollback, clone, passive backup, or relocation creates neither
lineage nor allowance merely by existing. Operational realization and local lock
are evidence, not lineage identity. Continuity must be positively established.
One active authority per lineage is required; active-active database copies are forbidden.
Missing, ambiguous, foreign, stale, conflicting, unreadable, or unfenced lineage,
continuity, admission, sole-active, or foreign-claim posture fails closed for every
applicable command. Local row absence never proves an unclaimed scope.

At most one distinct productive candidate acceptance may commit across all programs in one admitted scope.
The acceptance fact is permanent. Reset, renew, reopen, replenish, delete, or
recreate is forbidden, including a new grant or policy epoch. Replacement,
restore, rollback, clone, passive backup, and relocation never replenish allowance.
Independently admitted lineages may each have one distinct scope.

One immutable scope-admission fact binds the finite roles below. External standing
policy authors the admission act. The target recording operation is owned by
`.docs/rw0-c2-scope-admission-provisioning-contract.md`; physical and runtime
contracts remain future. Decision writer,
reducer, caller, model, replay, and recovery cannot admit, create, replace, renew,
or repair admission. Schema installation, empty or copied databases, and missing
local rows do not admit scope. Admission grants no branch, execution, runtime,
publication, Git, or ES authority.

## Command relation and eligibility

Every applicable command requires exact admitted lineage, scope, admission, continuity, sole-active authority, and no foreign claim.
Canonical expectations are non-authoritative, covered by command digest and
complete-family recovery selector, sufficient for one exact scope derivation and
stale/foreign/substituted/ambiguous expectation conflict detection. They cannot
select, admit, mint, renew, or replenish scope or request acceptance. Deterministic
family routes remain collision/recovery coordinates. Mutable selector, expected
available state/revision, preimage digest, and consumed-postimage assignment are
superseded mandatory architecture.

Acceptance alone requires exhaustive unclaimed-scope proof and all ordinary C2 preconditions.
This means exhaustive guard-family read, zero committed acceptance facts, zero
duplicate, alias, collision, partial, malformed, foreign, or unreadable families,
and exact command-family route. Local absence receives no credit. Universal
posture failure or acceptance eligibility failure is
`bootstrap_acceptance_guard_conflict`: no-write, never durable block.
Nonaccept branches create no acceptance fact and make no unclaimed-scope assertion.
Unknown claimed status alone may permit nonaccept only when universal posture,
authoritative derivation, and every other precondition are exact and derivation
does not rely on unclaimed status. Nonaccept never initializes or repairs guard truth.

## Immutable acceptance family and digest order

Only derived candidate acceptance creates one immutable acceptance-fact family member.
It binds the finite logical roles below, including the consuming mutation,
canonical command digest, exact acceptance decision, version/head, selected
attempt/candidate, immutable basis, family route, and one transaction timestamp.
It has no mutable successor. The future guarded decision transaction is the sole
acceptance-fact writer. There is no second guard transaction or separate guard receipt.

The digest graph is acyclic: admission act, admission fact, command expectations
and digest, decision/version/head, acceptance fact, result, receipt, replay and
whole-family recovery. Acceptance may bind decision/version/head, and result/
receipt may bind acceptance identity/digest. Acceptance never requires final
result/receipt digests; command digest never requires timestamp or postcommit projection.

Universal lineage/admission/continuity/sole-active posture is part of the command
and writer admission/fencing prefix, before receipt lookup. Replay does not
reevaluate fresh claimed status. Command-local grammar/basis and writer fencing
precede receipt-first replay and
collision checks, then authoritative branch derivation and acceptance-only
eligibility, construction/application of the atomic family, capability invalidation,
one commit attempt, and readback-only uncertainty resolution. Existing ordinary
command/basis/current-head reasons retain their owner; identity/route collision
uses `preassigned_identity_or_family_route_collision`, and same mutation with a
different digest uses `mutation_ref_reuse_conflict`. No overlapping reason is added.

Exact replay returns the original complete family and fact without reevaluation or a new timestamp.
Receipt-first lookup precedes fresh eligibility; historical mutable heads need
not remain fresh. The decision owner alone classifies the complete family.
Every expected immutable member exact is `committed_exact`. Original command's
new family exhaustively absent without fragment, alias, collision, or read fault
is `definitely_not_committed`. Otherwise classification is `unresolved`.
Decision without fact or fact without decision is unresolved.
A competing complete winner can prove original-family absence but never permits fresh retry in the claimed scope.
Recovery creates no decision, fact, timestamp, branch reevaluation, fragment repair,
scope change, or renewal; unresolved never permits automatic retry.

## Terminal, ES, and compatibility boundaries

A later terminal fact binds exact acceptance identity/digest and committed decision/result/receipt without guard mutation.
Absent terminal recording does not uncommit acceptance. Unresolved acceptance
family forbids terminal inference; terminal uncertainty leaves acceptance permanent.
Terminal cannot create admission/acceptance, consume, reset, update, delete, renew,
repair, or replenish truth. Terminal storage/writer/receipt/recovery are unselected.
Later ES operation is separately governed and never consumes, replenishes, resets, or reinterprets direct-local allowance.
Acceptance is no general ES admission/runtime authority. ES readiness, new grant,
policy epoch, restored database, or new machine creates no direct-local allowance.

The B1 legacy pair is representation/coherence only and is not current durable guard proof.
The exact names `latch_postimage_ref` and `latch_postimage_digest` remain a bounded
v1 compatibility edge with unchanged source, schema, field order, nullability,
canonical bytes, digest domain, and budgets. Replacement-target binding is
immutable acceptance-fact identity/digest, implying no mutable preimage/state/
transition/revision or separate classifier. B1 proves no scope, admission,
uniqueness, commitment, atomic membership, or recovery. A new target consumer is
gated on a separately accepted safe-value compatibility/versioning decision.
Rename/removal, field grammar, or proof responsibility requires another tranche.

Physical representation, provisioning, readers, writers, safe values, migration treatment, and runtime remain unimplemented and unselected.
No source, schema, migration, provisioner, writer, reader, recovery path, production
caller, public surface, runtime, RW0-D/E, or ES change is added. Current migrations
and source remain untouched. Full source preservation belongs to existing
structural guards and repository provenance; this owner adds no source manifest.
